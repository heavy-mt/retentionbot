The first three stages completed redaction and invalidation for all messages;
native purge removed the original messages. Queues drained. No new deadlocks
were observed at 10/25/50 msg/s. These are short burst tests, not sustained capacity proofs.
The initial 10 msg/s SQL samples were lost because `docker exec` lacked `-i`;
the monitor was corrected and observation resumed without another send run.

100 msg/s failed the requested rate and retention deadline. DAG tips grew during
message ingestion: 565 at t=12.8 s, 1268 at t=26.0 s, and 4588 at t=118.8 s.
At that point all 6000 messages had been accepted, with no new redactions.
Redaction began at approximately t=157 s. At t=304.7 s the job queue contained
5732 ready jobs and four unacknowledged jobs. One additional database deadlock
was observed. Original messages and retention jobs remained outstanding.

By the next observation at 19:18 Moscow time the system had recovered without
restart: queue empty, DAG tips 2, no original messages persisted, all 8550
invalidations done across the four stages. In the 100 msg/s stage, 4477 messages
were redacted and 1523 were purged before redaction. The exact recovery time was
not measured because observation stopped after the initial five-minute window.

## Findings and follow-up

The running module hash matched the server checkout. The high-rate DAG growth
started before redaction, so it is separate from the corrected historical
redaction branch behavior.

Inspection of the running Synapse 1.162.0 handler showed the per-room limiter
covering transaction lookup only. Its purge read lock and event creation call
were outside the limiter. The build patch moves that existing block inside the
limiter, preserving the send arguments and the purge read lock. It fails closed
for unrecognized handler layouts and is idempotent for the corrected layout.
Runtime verification must repeat 100 msg/s after deploying the CI-tested image;
the correction has not yet passed a server load test.

Serialization conflicts were also logged in `retention_invalidation_bump` and
`retention_cache_reset_queue_room`. Those operations eventually completed;
contention remains a separate optimization item.

After the high-rate test, `stream_ordering_to_exterm` had approximately 12.49
million live rows and total relation size 1697 MB, including earlier tests.
Without a per-room baseline its growth cannot be attributed precisely to this
run. Root free space decreased from approximately 7.46 to 5.63 GiB. No history
tables were manually deleted or vacuumed to hide the result.
